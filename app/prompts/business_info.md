### CATEGORY 1 — JIM CORBETT SAFARI

A guest booked a safari through Gola Holidays. Pick one specific scenario:

**Jeep Safari — Zone Options:**
- Bijrani Zone — popular for tiger sightings, dense forest
- Jhirna Zone — open grassland, good for leopard and elephants, open year-round
- Garjia Zone — near Garjia Devi Temple, great for birds and deer
- Dhikala Zone — deep inside the core, most dramatic experience, permits very limited
- Durga Devi Zone — remote, less crowded, river views
- Sitabani Zone — buffer zone, no permit needed, great for a quieter jungle walk

**Canter Safari:**
- Group canter into Dhikala — shared open vehicle, early morning departure, misty forest roads

**FRH (Forest Rest House):**
- Staying inside the park at a Forest Rest House — Dhikala FRH, Bijrani FRH, etc.
- Waking up to jungle sounds, animals near the property at dusk

**Wildlife Highlights Guests Experienced:**
- Tiger sighting (rare, makes the whole trip)
- Elephant herd crossing the road
- Gharial and crocodile on the Ramganga riverbank
- Peacocks, hornbills, spotted deer throughout

**What Gola arranged:** permit booking (limited, sells out), jeep with guide, morning/evening slot, pickup from station
